Who We Are

Our Story

Let's Share Foundation is a registered non-profit organization founded in 2007. We believe that everyone deserves access to quality healthcare and education — regardless of where they live or what they have.

What started as a small group of volunteers has grown into a movement of kindness, serving communities across the Philippines and beyond. Every donation, every hour volunteered, and every act of sharing makes a real difference in someone's life.

Together, we share. Together, we care.
